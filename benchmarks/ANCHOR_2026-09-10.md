# Flagship anchor — 2026-09-10

**Measured strength: ~2520 Elo vs native Stockfish @ 10+0.1** (interpolated 50% crossing;
per-anchor implied strengths bracket 2476–2549). This is an engineering anchor against
`UCI_LimitStrength` Stockfish, not a human/FIDE rating.

> **Correction (2026-09-10):** the first version of `bench_anchor.py` counted a draw with
> the candidate as Black as a win (`(result=="1-0") == (color=="white")` is True for a
> draw with color "black"). The table below is the corrected recount from the same PGNs;
> the ~2550 figure first reported was ~30 Elo high. The harness now excludes draws from
> both decisive counts.

## Method

- Engine: `target/release/uci.exe` built from `master` `c289071` (all promoted defaults:
  `loglmr`, `seeprune`, `caphist`, `improving`, `tt2`, `king_activity`), 1 thread,
  `--futility --rfp --tt-prune-store --qtt --histmalus --histlmr --lmp --smarttime`,
  net `target-cvs/matrix-raw.json` + helper `matrix-residual.json`.
- Opponent: local native Stockfish (`stockfish-windows-x86-64-avx2`),
  `option.UCI_LimitStrength=true`, `option.UCI_Elo=<anchor>`.
- 200 games per anchor over **100 distinct positions** (`openings-inv1-20260910.epd`,
  consumed sequentially — no position repeats within a run), colour-paired, tc=10+0.1,
  draw/resign adjudication, 10 concurrent games.
- Harness: `benchmarks/scripts/bench_anchor.py`; raw PGNs under
  `benchmarks/results/anchor-20260910/`.

## Results

| SF anchor | W-L-D | score | implied strength | 95% CI on the score |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2000 | 163-29-8 | 83.5% | ~2282 | [78.0%, 87.8%] |
| 2200 | 141-49-10 | 73.0% | ~2373 | [66.5%, 78.7%] |
| 2400 | 108-65-27 | 60.8% | ~2476 | [53.9%, 67.4%] |
| 2600 | 76-105-19 | 42.8% | ~2549 | [36.1%, 49.7%] |

The implied strength drifts upward with the anchor (2282 → 2549), which is the known
non-linearity of Stockfish's skill-limited mode; the **50% crossing (~2520)** is the
meaningful figure, with an honest bracket of roughly **2470–2560**. This is consistent
with, and slightly below, the README's historical ~2525–2535 band (which came from
20-game samples; this is 800).

## What the 800 games say

| game length | W-L-D | score |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 20 plies | 30-5-2 | 84% |
| 21–60 plies | 233-24-5 | 90% |
| 61–120 plies | 197-99-15 | 66% |
| **> 120 plies** | **28-120-42** | **26%** |

The engine wins the short and middlegame-heavy games overwhelmingly and **loses the long
games badly** — 190 games (24% of the sample) ran past 120 plies and scored 26%. That is
the same endgame/technique weakness the Lichess harvest showed (blunders 5× more often
when up material). Two of the 800 games were time forfeits (10+0.1 with adjudication).

Terminations: 555 adjudicated, 2 time forfeits, the rest decided on the board. Score by
colour was near-symmetric (as White 63.6%, as Black 66.8%), so the long-game losses are
not a colour artefact.

## Reading

- The historical claim in the README of a "~2525–2535 blitz band" is **reproduced and
  slightly exceeded** — that band was measured on 20-game samples, this on 800 games
  over 100 positions.
- The 2026-09-09/10 promotions are in this build (`loglmr` alone measured +35 Elo at
  fixed nodes; the unproven five are neutral-to-small). The bot's live failures that
  started this investigation were time-management, not engine strength: at 10+0.1 the
  engine plays ~2550, while the Lichess bot was starving it to 80–235 ms/move.
- Speed caveat: this anchor is 1 thread. The live bot runs 4 threads + 2 specialist
  lanes and `--smarttime`, so its practical strength is a little higher when its clock
  budget is sane.
