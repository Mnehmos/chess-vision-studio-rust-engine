# Cross-Generation Baseline: 2026-06-19

## Scope

This is a diagnostic fixed-depth baseline, not a promotion result or Elo
estimate. All nine registered engine identities passed artifact validation and
start-position smoke at depth 2.

This leaked-suite matrix is superseded for model comparison by
`CLEAN_BASELINE_2026-06-19.md`.

The decision matrix used `suite-fresh-100`, engine depth 8, one thread, and
Stockfish depth 12 child scoring. The suite is not clean promotion evidence:
47 of its 100 positions were used in Gen8-v2 training, and overlap with Gen9
training data has not been fully excluded.

and game evidence. Selectivity options must be reintroduced one at a time and
tested at equal time before any default promotion.
